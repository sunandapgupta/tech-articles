How Big IT Companies Really Work

This article explores the inner workings of large IT companies, with insights from Scandinavian tech organizations.

Key highlights:

Balance over perfection – Success comes from balancing people, technology, and trust, not following rigid processes.

Workflow reality – Development is iterative, with pauses and refinements considered wisdom, not inefficiency.

Tools support conversation – Common tools (Jira, GitHub, Slack, Azure, Selenium, etc.) enable collaboration rather than replace human communication.

Collaboration is the architecture – Cross-functional teams (product, development, QA, design, operations) work together from the start, supported by low hierarchy and psychological safety.

Flexible processes – Agile frameworks (Scrum, Kanban) are adapted thoughtfully, emphasizing feedback, alignment, and continuous learning.

Sustained success – Clear direction, team autonomy, cross-department collaboration, and user feedback loops drive long-term performance.

Human-centered scale – Daily conversations, shared responsibility, and mutual respect keep large IT systems functioning efficiently.
